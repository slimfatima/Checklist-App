Pre-submission curation checklist

Work through the four stages for each study before you ask a reviewer for feedback. An unchecked item doesn't mean the data is wrong. It means the entry still needs attention.

1. Verify the study metadata In progressComplete

Check the foundation of the record before looking at any signature.

2. Validate the experimental design In progressComplete

Make sure each experiment keeps the meaning of what the researchers compared.

3. Trace every signature to its evidence In progressComplete

If you can't trace an entry back to the paper, investigate before you submit.

4. Review before requesting feedback In progressComplete

Read metadata, groups, signatures and evidence as one connected whole.

All stages complete. This study is ready for review.

Unofficial helper based on the BugSigDB Curation Policy. It supports scientific judgment and peer review; it doesn't replace them. Open source under the MIT License.
